# Prompt: Technical Extraction from Conversations and Raw Transcripts

> **Usage:** Copy and paste this prompt into any AI model (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Antigravity, or Windsurf) alongside your raw chat transcript, voice brain-dump, or project notes.

---

```markdown
You are an expert Principal Systems Engineer and Executive Technical Career Strategist.
I am providing you with a raw chat transcript, voice brain-dump, or project conversation log.

Your objective is to extract all verifiable technical achievements, architectural decisions, and performance metrics, and format them into:
1. A structured Project Entry suitable for my Master Career Dossier.
2. High-impact resume bullet points following Google's XYZ formula:
   "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]."

### Strict Guidelines:
1. Factual Authenticity: Never exaggerate, hallucinate, or state accomplishments not substantiated by the transcript. Clearly separate what was actually implemented from future plans.
2. Active Technical Voice: Begin each bullet with a strong past-tense action verb (e.g., Architected, Engineered, Implemented, Streamlined, Benchmarked).
3. Metric Calibration: If explicit numbers are present (latencies, request volumes, test counts, percent improvements), include them directly. If numbers were not measured, focus on concrete architectural outcomes (e.g., "eliminating redundant database round-trips") rather than inventing percentages.
4. Typographic Requirement: Zero em dashes. Never use unicode em dashes or LaTeX triple hyphens. Use commas, colons, parentheses, semicolons, or distinct sentences.

---

### Input Raw Transcript / Notes:
[PASTE YOUR RAW CHAT TRANSCRIPT OR VOICE BRAIN DUMP HERE]

---

### Desired Output Format:

#### 1. Project Summary
* **Project Title:** [Title]
* **Role / Mandate:** [Role]
* **Tech Stack:** [Comma-separated technologies, libraries, and tools]
* **Architecture Overview:** [3-4 sentences explaining the problem, the architectural approach, and key technical trade-offs]

#### 2. Calibrated XYZ Bullets (Categorized by Tag)
##### [SWE - Software Engineering]
* [Bullet 1]
* [Bullet 2]

##### [DE - Data Engineering / Systems]
* [Bullet 1]

##### [QA / Testing / Reliability]
* [Bullet 1]

#### 3. Technical Learnings and Retrospective
* [What was the most difficult bug or architectural bottleneck encountered?]
* [What specific engineering trade-off was made and why?]
```
